We want everyone to be able to use Switched On, including with a screen reader, a keyboard only, or text made bigger. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA.
{: .answer}

If something on the site doesn't work for you, email [{{ site.email }}](mailto:{{ site.email }}) and tell us the page and what went wrong. We'll reply within two working days.

## What we've done

- **Plain English.** Every guide answers the question in its first two sentences, with no jargon.
- **Headings and landmarks.** Each page has one main heading and a clear order of headings below it, so a screen reader can jump between sections. A "Skip to content" link comes first.
- **Keyboard.** Everything works with a keyboard, and the item you're on always shows a thick outline.
- **Colour.** Text colours are checked against their backgrounds (at least 4.5 to 1 for normal text). Links in text are underlined, so they don't rely on colour alone.
- **Zoom and small screens.** Pages fit a screen 320 pixels wide, the same as 400% zoom on a laptop, without scrolling sideways. On a phone, wide price tables scroll sideways inside the page.
- **Diagrams.** Every diagram has a full description in words, which screen readers read out instead of the drawing. Everything a diagram shows is also in the guide's text.
- **Tables** have header rows, and **forms** have a visible label on every field.
- **Nothing moves or pops up.** No autoplay video, no moving banners, no pop-ups and no cookie banner (we don't use cookies).

## What we know isn't perfect

- **Diagrams and your own text spacing.** The words in a diagram are part of the drawing. If you set your browser to add extra space between letters or words, some words near the edge of a diagram may be cut off. The description and the guide's text carry the same information.
- **Other websites.** Guides link to providers and public bodies. We can't control how accessible their pages are.
- **Screen reader checks.** Our checks so far are automated checks of every page type plus keyboard checks. If you use a screen reader and something reads oddly, please tell us.

## How we check

We check every type of page (the home page, topic pages, guides and these pages) before launch and after any change to the site's design. The checks cover headings, landmarks, the skip link, link and button names, form labels, colour contrast, keyboard focus, target sizes, small screens and text spacing.
