Email your question to [{{ site.email }}](mailto:{{ site.email }}?subject=Question%20for%20Switched%20On). We can't reply to every email, but the questions readers ask most become guides, researched from Irish sources and checked before they go up.
{: .answer}

## What to send

- Your question, in a sentence or two
- Anything that changes the answer, such as your network or broadband provider, the model of phone or laptop, or where you bought it

Please don't send account numbers, passwords or other private details.

## What happens next

1. John Paul reads the questions.
2. If it isn't covered already, it may become a guide. We research the answer from Irish sources, such as the company's own Irish pages or a public body like ComReg, the CCPC or Revenue.
3. If your question becomes a guide, we'll try to email you the link. We don't publish your name unless you ask us to.

## What we can't help with

- **Your own complaint or dispute.** We can't take up individual cases or give legal advice. For a faulty item, see [Your rights when tech is faulty in Ireland]({{ '/buying/faulty-tech-rights/' | relative_url }}).
- **TV and streaming.** Our sister site, [{{ site.sister_site.name }}]({{ site.sister_site.url }}), covers these.
- **Anything unlicensed.** We only cover legal products and services.

We use your email address only to reply to your question. See our [privacy notice]({{ '/privacy/' | relative_url }}).
