Switched On is an independent guide to everyday tech in Ireland: phones, broadband, computers, apps and buying tech here. It's run by [John Paul Cunningham]({{ '/about/john-paul-cunningham/' | relative_url }}) in Co. Kilkenny.
{: .answer}

We answer what global sites don't: what works here, what it costs here and where to get it here. TV and streaming are covered by our sister site, [{{ site.sister_site.name }}]({{ site.sister_site.url }}).

## Why I started Switched On

> I started Switched On to help Irish households find useful information about technology that works in Ireland: how to set it up and use it, what it costs and where to get it here, and your rights if something goes wrong. Every guide is meant to be easy to follow.
>
> John Paul Cunningham
