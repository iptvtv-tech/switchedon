At the moment Switched On earns nothing from its guides: there are no affiliate links, no ads and no sponsored posts. John Paul Cunningham pays the running costs himself.
{: .answer}

## What never affects a guide

- **No company pays to be covered.** We don't publish sponsored posts or paid reviews, and no company sees a guide before it goes live.
- **No free products.** We don't accept products to review. Our guides are researched from published sources, not tested, and we say so on every guide.
- **Source links never earn us money.** Every guide ends with a list of the sources it's based on, such as ComReg, Revenue and the companies' own Irish pages. Those links are plain links.

## If we add affiliate links

Later, some buying guides may include affiliate links. If you buy through one, the shop pays us a small commission, at no extra cost to you. If we start doing this:

- This page will name every affiliate programme we belong to.
- Any guide with affiliate links will start with a box labelled **Ad**, saying the guide has affiliate links.
- Every link that can earn us a commission will be marked "(paid link)".
- Product cards will appear only in buying guides, chosen from our research, with at most three in a guide.
- Links won't change what a guide says or which products it picks.
