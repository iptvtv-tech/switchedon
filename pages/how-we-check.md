Every guide is researched from Irish and EU primary sources, checked before it goes live and checked again every three months. We don't test products ourselves, and we say so on every guide.
{: .answer}

## Where our facts come from

Prices, availability and rules come from the company's own Irish page, or from an Irish or EU public body such as ComReg, the CCPC or Revenue. We don't use comparison sites as sources, and source links never earn us money. Every guide lists its sources, with the date each one was checked.

## How guides are written

Each guide is researched and drafted with the help of an AI assistant, working only from the sources listed on the guide. John Paul Cunningham then checks every fact against those sources, edits the guide and approves it before it goes live. We publish one or two guides a week, never in bulk.

## What "researched, not tested" means

We read what the maker, the network or the regulator publishes, and what's actually available in Ireland. We don't have the products in our hands, so we never describe how something feels to use or claim to have tried it.

## Keeping guides up to date

Each guide is checked again at least every three months, and sooner when a price or rule changes. The "Last checked" date at the top of each guide shows when. When a check changes what a guide says, the newest change is shown at the top too, and every change is listed at the end of the guide.
