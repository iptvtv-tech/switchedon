Switched On sets no cookies and doesn't track you. We only hold your details if you email us, sign up to the newsletter or follow our WhatsApp channel, and we use them only for that.
{: .answer}

## Who we are

Switched On is run by John Paul Cunningham, Windgap, Co. Kilkenny, Ireland, who is the data controller for this site. Email [{{ site.email }}](mailto:{{ site.email }}) about anything on this page.

## What we collect and why

**Emails you send us.** If you send a question or a correction, we have your email address, your name if you include it, and your message. We use them only to read and reply to your email and, if your question becomes a guide, to tell you. We don't publish your name unless you ask us to. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in answering you and keeping our guides right.

**The newsletter.** If you sign up, we keep your email address and send you one email a month. We email you first to confirm you want it, and every email has an unsubscribe link. The legal basis is your consent, which you can withdraw at any time by unsubscribing. Question emails are never added to the newsletter list.

**Visit counts.** We count visits with [GoatCounter](https://www.goatcounter.com/help/privacy), which doesn't use cookies or store anything in your browser. GoatCounter says it doesn't store IP addresses, the full browser details or any tracking ID, only totals such as which pages are read, the browser, the country and the site you came from. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in knowing which guides are useful.

**Our host's logs.** This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub says that when a GitHub Pages site is visited, the visitor's IP address is logged and stored for security purposes. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in keeping the site secure and working.

**Our WhatsApp channel.** If you follow it, WhatsApp handles your information under its own privacy policy. WhatsApp says your phone number stays private, and only channel admins who have you saved as a contact can see it.

## What we don't do

- No cookies, so there's no cookie banner.
- No advertising, no Google Analytics and no tracking across other sites.
- Our fonts are stored on our own site, so loading a page doesn't contact Google.
- We never sell or share your details for marketing.

## Who else handles your data

- **GitHub** (GitHub, Inc., United States) hosts the site. See [GitHub's Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement), including how it protects data transferred from the EU.
- **GoatCounter**, run from Ireland on servers in Finland and Germany, counts visits.
- **Brevo** (France) stores the newsletter list and sends the emails for us. Brevo says data may be transferred to countries including the United States and India, with safeguards in place. See [Brevo's privacy policy](https://www.brevo.com/legal/privacypolicy/).
- **Proton** (Proton AG, Switzerland) hosts our email, including the questions and corrections you send us. The EU recognises Switzerland as giving personal data an adequate level of protection. See [Proton's privacy policy](https://proton.me/legal/privacy).
- **WhatsApp** runs our channel. See [WhatsApp's Channels privacy policy](https://www.whatsapp.com/legal/channels-privacy-policy-eea/).

## How long we keep it

- **Emails you send us:** deleted within 12 months of our last reply, or sooner if you ask.
- **Newsletter sign-ups:** until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them.
- **Visit counts:** kept as totals, with nothing that identifies you.
- **GitHub's logs:** kept by GitHub under its own policy.

## Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of the information we hold about you, and ask us to correct it, delete it or stop using it. You can also object to how we use it, ask us to restrict it or ask for it in a format you can take elsewhere. Where you gave consent, such as for the newsletter, you can withdraw it at any time.

Email [{{ site.email }}](mailto:{{ site.email }}) and we'll reply within one month.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, you can raise it with the Data Protection Commission using its [online form](https://forms.dataprotection.ie/contact), or by post to 6 Pembroke Row, Dublin 2, D02 X963. The Commission usually asks that you contact us first.
