You can get a verified MyGovID on your phone with the free MyGovID app, if you're 16 or over, live in Ireland, have a valid Irish passport book and haven't been through SAFE registration before. If the app approves you, your Public Services Card comes in the post.
{: .answer}

Already have a Public Services Card? You can verify your account on MyGovID.ie with your card number, its expiry date and a code sent to your phone.
{: .answer}

{% include key-points.html %}

## Basic or verified?

MyGovID is a single login for government services, run by the Department of Social Protection. There are two types of account: basic and verified.

A basic account needs only your name and an email address. It lets you book an appointment for a PPS number or a Public Services Card.

Many services need a verified account, including Revenue's myAccount, SUSI grant applications, driver licence applications, the National Childcare Scheme and MyWelfare. You can't log in to the HSE Health App with a basic account.

## Step 1: create a basic account

1. Go to MyGovID.ie and click "Create an Account Now" at the top right, or "Get a Basic Account Now" on the home page.
2. Enter your full name, email address and a password, tick the box to confirm you're over 16, and click "Create account".
3. Enter the 6-digit code sent to your email.
4. Read the privacy statement and terms, and click "I agree".

## If you have a Public Services Card: verify online

1. At MyGovID.ie, click "Login with MyGovID" at the top right and log in.
2. Click "Verify your account" at the top right.
3. Enter your PPS number, then your card number and expiry date.
4. Enter the 6-digit code sent to the mobile number linked to your card.

An out-of-date card can still be used to set up MyGovID.

## If you don't: use the MyGovID app

The app is free from the App Store and Google Play, and comes from the Department of Social Protection. It's for people who live in Ireland, have a current Irish passport, are 16 or over, have a basic MyGovID account and haven't been through SAFE registration before.

### What you need

- A valid Irish passport book. Passport cards aren't accepted.
- A phone with a front camera and NFC. The App Store listing needs iOS 18.6 or later; gov.ie says Android 10 or later.
- Your Apple or Google account set to Ireland as its location.
- Your PPS number.
- If your address differs from the department's records, proof of address dated within the last 6 months, such as a utility bill or bank statement. It's deleted after processing.

### The steps

1. Log in to the app with your basic account and enter your PPS number.
2. Take a selfie. It goes on your Public Services Card.
3. Photograph your passport's details page and scan its chip with your phone.
4. Add your signature, your birth surname, your mother's birth surname and your address.
5. Choose two security questions and enter your mobile number.

### What happens next

If you're approved, you get a verified MyGovID, and your Public Services Card comes by post to the address you gave. If the application can't be processed, you're told what to bring to a Public Services Card centre.

## If you can't use the app

Get a Public Services Card in person, then verify online as above. The quickest way is to contact your local Public Services Card centre. If you don't have email, the National Intreo Contact Centre can arrange it by phone, and some centres take walk-ins.

Bring photo ID (for an Irish citizen born in Ireland, a current Irish passport or an Irish or UK driving licence), proof of address dated within the last 6 months, your mobile phone and your PPS number. Getting a card can take a few days.

## The Government Digital Wallet is a different app

The Government Digital Wallet is a separate app for storing verified digital credentials, and you sign in to it with MyGovID. It's in a consultation and testing phase, and using it is voluntary. For when you can get it and what it will hold, see [What is Ireland's Government Digital Wallet?]({{ '/apps/government-digital-wallet/' | relative_url }})
