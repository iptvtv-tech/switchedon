Yes. Many Apple Intelligence features work in Ireland on a supported iPhone, iPad or Mac once you update to iOS 27, iPadOS 27 or macOS 27, with your device and Siri set to the same supported language, such as English.
{: .answer}

The exception is Siri AI (Beta), Apple's new version of Siri. Apple says it isn't available on iPhone, iPad or Apple Watch in the EU, and it has no timeline for iPhone and iPad. In Ireland you can use it on a Mac or Apple Vision Pro.
{: .answer}

## What works in Ireland

Apple says the new Apple Intelligence features in iOS 27, iPadOS 27 and macOS 27 Golden Gate, such as advanced photo editing, the reimagined Image Playground and intelligent features in Safari, are available to people based in the EU.

Apple's list also includes features from iOS 26 onwards, such as Writing Tools, summaries in Mail and Messages, notification summaries, Genmoji, natural language search in Photos and Live Translation in Messages. It says some features may not be available in every region or language. Voicemail summaries and Notes transcription summaries both work in English (Ireland).

Once it's set up, Apple Intelligence keeps working when you travel abroad.

## Siri AI: on a Mac, not an iPhone

Siri AI (Beta) is a new version of Siri powered by Apple Intelligence. In the EU, Apple says it isn't available on iPhone or iPad, or on Apple Watch, which needs a paired iPhone with Siri AI. It is available on a Mac with macOS 27 Golden Gate and on Apple Vision Pro, if your Apple Account region is in the EU and your device and Siri languages match.

Apple says it has no timeline for Siri AI on iPhone and iPad in the EU.

On a Mac with M1 or later, or a MacBook Neo, update to macOS 27 Golden Gate, then go to Settings, click Siri and click Try Siri AI (Beta). At busy times you may be put on a waiting list. Siri AI works in English, French, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese and Spanish, and it isn't available to users under 13.

## Which devices and settings you need

- **iPhone:** iPhone 15 Pro or 15 Pro Max, iPhone 16 models or later, iPhone Air or iPhone Duo, with iOS 27
- **iPad:** iPad mini (A17 Pro or A20 Pro) or an iPad with M1 or later, with iPadOS 27
- **Mac:** a Mac with M1 or later, or MacBook Neo, with macOS 27 Golden Gate
- **Apple Watch:** Series 9 or later, Ultra 2 or later, or SE 3, paired with a supported iPhone

Apple Intelligence is included in the 27 software updates for these devices. It needs up to 8 GB of storage, or up to 14 GB on iPhone 17 Pro models and later, iPhone Air, iPhone Duo and some newer iPads and Macs.

Set your device language and Siri language to the same supported language; English is one. Apple says if you change the Siri language, Apple Intelligence may not work until the new language has downloaded.

## Limits and privacy

Some features that use Apple's servers have daily limits, including Siri AI, the photo editing tools and Image Playground. Apple says limits vary with demand and other factors, and that expanded access will be available for a fee in the future.

The ChatGPT extension works only where ChatGPT is available.

Apple says Apple Intelligence works on your device or through its Private Cloud Compute servers, without making your data accessible to Apple or anyone else.
