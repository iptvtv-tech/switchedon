Mostly because of Ireland's Online Safety Code. Since July 2025, video-sharing platforms regulated in Ireland that allow pornography or extreme violence, such as X, must use effective age checks so children can't normally see that content, and simply saying how old you are doesn't count.
{: .answer}

Some gaming platforms have their own checks too: PlayStation now needs adult accounts in Ireland to verify their age before using chat.
{: .answer}

## Which platforms it covers

The Online Safety Code covers the video-sharing platforms that Coimisiún na Meán, Ireland's media regulator, has designated. Its register lists nine: Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, Pinterest, TikTok, Tumblr, Udemy, X and YouTube.

The age-check rule applies to those that allow adult-only video, such as pornography or gross and gratuitous violence. On 8 September 2026, Coimisiún na Meán opened its first investigation under the code, into whether X's age checks and parental controls work.

## What counts as a check

Under the code, an age check based only on what you say about yourself, such as typing in your date of birth, isn't effective.

A platform that breaks the code can be fined up to 10% of its relevant annual turnover, or €20 million if that's more.

## PlayStation

PlayStation says age verification is now required for adult accounts in Ireland to use certain features. You only do it once per account.

Without it, you can still play games and use the PlayStation Store, but you can't use voice chat, text chat and messaging, join parties, use Discord voice chat through PlayStation, or broadcast to YouTube or Twitch. Some in-game chat may be blocked too.

You can verify with a facial scan, ID (a passport, driving licence or national ID card), or a mobile number on a contract in your name; pay as you go and prepay numbers don't work. The checks are done by Yoti or Incode, depending on availability, and the screens show which methods you can use. PlayStation says Yoti deletes facial scan data as soon as the check is finished.

Players under 18 need an account managed by a parent or guardian.

## Xbox

Microsoft's age verification page lists Australia, Brazil, Singapore and the UK, not Ireland. Where it applies, adults who don't verify can't buy or download new games and apps rated 18+.

## If you're a parent

Platforms covered by the code that allow users under 16 must have parental controls that work, are easy to find, and are pointed out when an account is set up.

## Questions or concerns

To ask about the rules, or raise a concern about a platform, contact Coimisiún na Meán on 01 963 7755 (Monday to Friday, 8am to 6pm) or at usersupport@cnam.ie.
