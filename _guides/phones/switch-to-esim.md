Three, Vodafone and eir all offer eSIM, and existing customers can swap a physical SIM for one on the same account.
{: .answer}

Check your phone supports eSIM first (it will have an EID number), then ask for the swap in your network's app, online or in a store, and activate it with Wi-Fi on.
{: .answer}

{% include key-points.html %}

## Check your phone first

- **Does it support eSIM?** eSIM only works on compatible phones, though Three says most newer phones are. eir says only phones that support eSIM have an EID number. To find it, dial \*#06#, or on an iPhone go to **Settings > General > About**. Three's and Vodafone's device guides also show which phones work.
- **Two networks on one phone?** Three says you need an unlocked phone to use plans from two networks, or to add a local data plan when you're abroad.
- **Bought your iPhone in the US?** Apple says iPhone 14 and later models bought in the United States support eSIM only. See [Will a phone bought abroad work in Ireland?]({{ '/phones/phone-bought-abroad/' | relative_url }})
- **A smartwatch?** eir doesn't currently support eSIM on smartwatches. Three offers a Smartwatch Share plan for a cellular Apple Watch.

## How to switch on each network

**Three**

- Existing customers can order a replacement eSIM online on Three's Replace my SIM page, by phone or in a Three store.
- If your phone supports On Device Activation, the eSIM sets itself up when you set up the phone. If not, Three emails you a QR code and instructions. Turn on Wi-Fi before you start.

**Vodafone**

- In My Vodafone, go to **My SIM** and select **Order a new SIM** or **eSIM QR code**, or ask in chat.
- To activate it you need Wi-Fi or mobile data, plus the QR code and activation PIN shown in My SIM. You can scan the code, or type the activation code and PIN in your phone's settings.
- If Vodafone can't verify you online, visit a store for an ID check. Vodafone's 1907 line can't give out QR codes or PINs.
- Vodafone says switching from a physical SIM to an eSIM doesn't change your number.

**eir**

- Ask for a SIM swap in the eir app or in an eir store. eir then emails you how to set up the eSIM with a QR code.
- The eir app shows the QR code and the passcode you'll need. eir suggests opening the app on another device, such as a laptop, so you can scan the code with the phone.
- Take out your physical SIM once the eSIM is working.

None of the three networks' eSIM pages lists a charge for switching, so check with yours before you start.

## Two numbers on one phone

On a dual SIM phone you can use a physical SIM and an eSIM together, with two numbers and two plans. eir notes you can store several eSIMs, but only two SIMs can be active at once.

## Moving your eSIM to a new phone

- **Three:** if both phones support On Device Transfer, the eSIM moves across during setup. If not, Three gives you a QR code and instructions.
- **Vodafone:** between iPhones, use Quick Transfer if both run iOS 18.2 or later; between Android phones, use Android's eSIM transfer. You can't move an eSIM directly between Android and iPhone, so ask Vodafone for a new eSIM profile.
- **eir:** moving an eSIM with the phone's own transfer tool isn't currently available. Delete the eSIM from the old phone first, then in the eir app go to **Manage SIM & eSIM**, tap **QR code & passcode** and scan the code with the new phone.

## If you lose your phone

- **Vodafone:** use chat or visit a store. Vodafone deactivates the old eSIM and issues a new one.
- **eir:** request a replacement in the eir app under **Manage SIM & eSIM**, or in any eir store.

## Joining a new network?

New customers can choose eSIM when they join Three, Vodafone or eir. To bring your number with you, see [How to keep your number when you switch mobile network]({{ '/phones/keep-your-number/' | relative_url }}).
