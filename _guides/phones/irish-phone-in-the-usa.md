Yes, but it costs extra, and for calls your phone needs to support 4G calling. The EU's 'Roam like at home' rules don't cover the USA, and the 3G networks there have closed.
{: .answer}

Vodafone's RED Roaming is €3.99 a day on Vodafone Unlimited plans and €2.99 a day on older plans, with calls and texts to Ireland and within the USA included. Three's Roaming Day Pass is €5.99 a day for 2GB of data, with calls and texts charged on top, and eir charges for calls by the minute and sells 1GB of US data for €29.99.
{: .answer}

{% include verdict.html %}

## Your phone needs 4G calling

Vodafone and eir say the 3G networks in the USA have closed, and Three says 2G and 3G calling there is being phased out. Vodafone says the 2G coverage that's left is limited, and eir says 2G voice services there were closing down from 3 August 2026. So for calls, your phone needs 4G calling.

Three says some older phones won't have service while roaming in the USA, and eir advises against travelling with a phone that only has 2G or 3G. eir adds that the US networks' 4G works only with selected phones.

Before you go, check that your phone has 4G calling and that it's set up: Three's online device guides show whether a phone supports it. For more on 4G calling, see [Will a phone bought abroad work in Ireland?]({{ '/phones/phone-bought-abroad/' | relative_url }})

## What Vodafone, Three and eir charge

{% include price-date.html note="They can change, so check your network's own roaming page before you travel." %}

- **Vodafone:** RED Roaming costs €3.99 a day on Vodafone Unlimited plans and €2.99 a day on older bill pay and pay as you go plans. On Vodafone Unlimited that gets you unlimited calls and texts to Ireland and within the USA, plus 500MB of data a day, and extra data costs 6c per MB. Some older plans use your home allowance instead, so check Vodafone's roaming page. You pay only on days you use your phone there: the fee starts with your first call made or received, text sent or data used, and the day runs to 11:59pm Irish time.
- **Three:** the Roaming Day Pass costs €5.99 a day for 2GB of data, then 6c per MB. It's data only, so calls and texts are charged at your plan's roaming rates: enter your number and the country in the rate checker on Three's roaming page to see them. Once the pass is on your account, it starts when your phone first uses mobile data abroad (not WiFi), and it renews after midnight Irish time the next time you use data. A 3-day pass with 6GB costs €14.99 on bill pay or €15 on prepay, and a 7-day pass with 14GB costs €34.99 or €35.
- **eir:** on bill pay, calls cost €1.72 a minute to make and €1.62 a minute to receive, and texts are 40c. On prepay, calls are €1.99 a minute either way and texts are 39c. Data is €4.99 per MB on both, or you can buy 1GB of US data for €29.99 by texting Data USACAN to 50104.

Vodafone Unlimited customers get RED Roaming automatically. On older Vodafone bill pay plans, text RED to 50020 to opt in. Vodafone pay as you go customers who joined from July 2017 have it already; if you joined earlier, text RED to 50020, and keep enough credit for the daily fee.

Other networks set their own US charges, so check your network's roaming page or app, or ask it, before you travel.

## Keep data costs down

Your data roaming has a preset limit of €61.50, including VAT. ComReg, the communications regulator, says you should get a text when you've spent €49.20, another at €61.50, when you must opt in to keep using data, and another at €123. To go past the limit, Three says to text REMCAP to 50232, and Vodafone says to text PASS CAP to 50193. Either way, you then pay for the extra data.

The alerts can arrive late, and they cover data only, not calls or texts. If your network can't send them in the USA, you should get a message saying so, and you're responsible for the bill.

If you don't need mobile data, turn off data roaming in your phone's settings before you arrive: you can still use WiFi. If you do use data, set your email and apps not to sync in the background, as Vodafone advises, and keep an eye on your phone's data counter.

## WiFi Calling in the USA

Of Vodafone, Three and eir, only eir's WiFi Calling works abroad. Calls to Irish numbers come out of your allowance, but calls to US numbers are charged at eir's international rates from Ireland; to pay roaming rates for those instead, eir says to turn WiFi Calling off. Vodafone's WiFi Calling isn't available when you're roaming, and Three's works in the Republic of Ireland only. See [Which Irish networks support WiFi Calling?]({{ '/phones/wifi-calling-networks/' | relative_url }})

## An eSIM or a US SIM instead

Instead of roaming, you can buy a travel eSIM: you usually pay another provider upfront for a plan based on where you're going, how much data you need and for how long, such as 3GB for 3 days in the USA. Some are data only; others include calls and texts, with a second phone number. Follow the eSIM provider's set-up steps carefully, or your phone may keep using your Irish network.

Or buy a US SIM card when you arrive. Your phone must be unlocked, and you then pay local rates instead of roaming rates.

## Emergencies and a lost phone

The emergency number in the USA is 911, and calls to it are free. When you arrive, you should get a text reminding you of the local emergency number.

If your phone is lost or stolen, contact your network straight away with your phone's IMEI number: thieves can run up bills you may have to pay.
