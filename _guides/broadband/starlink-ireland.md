Yes. Starlink's map shows the whole of Ireland as available, and its home plans cost €35, €55 or €75 a month, with the dish and router rented free. The two cheaper plans are only offered in some areas, so enter your address on Starlink's Irish site to see what you can get.

## What it costs

{% include price-date.html note="They can change, so check Starlink's own page before you order." %}

| Plan | Monthly price | Top download speed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Residential 100 Mbps | €35 | 100 Mbps |
| Residential 200 Mbps | €55 | 200 Mbps |
| Residential Max | €75 | 400 Mbps |

Prices include VAT. There's no activation fee, and the shipping charge depends on where you live.

Home plans come with the Starlink kit on free rental: the dish, Wi-Fi router, power supply and mounts. If you cancel, you must return it in good condition within 30 days, or you'll be charged the full kit price. Starlink provides a return label.

For use away from home, Starlink's Roam plans cost €45 a month for 100GB or €95 for unlimited data, and you buy the kit: €199 for the Mini or €349 for the Standard kit.

To see whether fibre broadband can reach your home too, see our guide to [checking for fibre at your address]({{ '/broadband/fibre-at-my-address/' | relative_url }}).

## Speeds

Starlink's Irish contract summary lists 80 Mbps download and 15 Mbps upload as the normally available speed on all three home plans, with latency (delay) of 25 to 50 milliseconds. Residential Max gets the fastest available speeds and top priority among the home plans.

Starlink says speeds aren't guaranteed and can be slower at busy times.

## Trying it and cancelling

Starlink offers a 30-day trial: if you're not satisfied, you can get a full refund, subject to its return conditions.

Separately, you can cancel within 14 days of signing up without giving a reason. Starlink says this is in addition to the 30-day trial. If you asked for the service to start within those 14 days, you pay for what you used.

After that, the contract runs month to month, and you can cancel at any time in your Starlink account with no penalty or fee.

## Getting help and complaining

Starlink's contact route is a support ticket in your account on starlink.com or in the Starlink app.

To make a complaint, you can open a ticket, write to Starlink Internet Services Limited in Dublin, or phone 1800 458 085. Starlink says it acknowledges complaints within 2 business days and responds within 10.

If the service keeps failing because of a fault on Starlink's network, you can ask Starlink to end your contract without penalty or reduce your monthly price.

If you're not happy with the outcome, or 10 working days have passed, you can take it to ComReg, the communications regulator: see "If something goes wrong" in our guide to [switching broadband provider]({{ '/broadband/switch-provider/' | relative_url }}#if-something-goes-wrong).
